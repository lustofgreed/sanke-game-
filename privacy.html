Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025. Zmey Gorynych Royal is committed to keeping things simple and transparent.

Overview

Zmey Gorynych Royal is a browser-based game. In its current form, it does not use advertising, analytics tracking, account systems, or a backend server. The game stores a small amount of progress data in your own browser.

What data we store

The game saves the following information locally in your browser using the browser's storage (localStorage):

  • Your chosen player name.
  • Your selected skin and unlocked skins.
  • Your coins balance and best length.
  • Your mute setting and graphics quality setting.

This data never leaves your device. It is used only to keep your progress and preferences between visits.

What we do NOT collect

  • No account or login information.
  • No email address is collected by the game itself.
  • No personal information is sent to us automatically.
  • No tracking cookies are set by the game.

Third-party services

If you contact us by email, you voluntarily provide your email address, which we use only to respond to you.

As we continue to build the site, we may add third-party services. If we later add advertising (for example, Google AdSense) or analytics, this policy will be updated to describe what those services collect and how you can control it. Any such advertising will follow Google's advertising policies, and you can learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies

The game itself does not use cookies. Third-party services we add in the future (such as advertising partners) may use cookies or similar technologies to personalise or measure ads. If and when that happens, we will clearly update this policy and, where required, provide you with choices.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game is designed for general audiences and does not ask for personal details.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at gamexcreator.com@gmail.com.
